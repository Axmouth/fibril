Every change here was measured against the build before it. Some were clear
wins, some became settings because they trade one thing for another, and some
lost and were removed. Each chart starts at zero, with the earlier build in
grey and the change in colour: green where it helped, red where it cost.

Unless an entry says otherwise, numbers come from three brokers sharing one
consumer NVMe drive with durable 1 KiB messages, two or more runs per arm
alternating, and ranges are run to run. Dates are when the measurement was
taken. The [optimization log](/development/optimization-log/) holds the full
rows with commits and conditions, and the
[storage write path](/development/storage-write-path/) animates the storage
changes.
